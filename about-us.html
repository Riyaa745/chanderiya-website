Our story

Serving institutions with purpose since 2011.

Celebrating 15 yearsBuilding trust since 2011

Chanderiya Marketing and Sales Company was established in 2011 by Mr. R. K. Chanderiya. We are a manufacturer and service provider dedicated to high-quality products and reliable business solutions.

We specialize in commercial workspace infrastructure, modular storage, institutional furniture, fire safety solutions and industrial water purification. Our customers include government enterprises, public sector units, banks and private institutions.

At Chanderiya Marketing and Sales, we believe that long-term relationships are built on trust, transparency, and consistent performance. Our experienced team is committed to providing timely deliveries, responsive support, and professional service, making us a preferred choice for customers and business partners alike.

As we continue to expand our presence, our focus remains on innovation, operational excellence, and customer satisfaction. We are dedicated to continuously improving our product portfolio and services while maintaining the highest ethical standards in every aspect of our business.
