Privacy Policy

How we handle information shared through this website and our enquiry channels.

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Chanderiya Marketing and Sales respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you contact us and how it is used.

Information we receive

When you submit an enquiry, call, email or contact us on WhatsApp, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, organization, location, product interests and project or service requirements.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotations.
  • To arrange product supply, delivery, installation or maintenance.
  • To provide customer and after-sales support.
  • To maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.

WhatsApp and external platforms

Website enquiry forms open WhatsApp with a prepared message. The website does not itself store the form submission. Information sent through WhatsApp, email, Facebook, LinkedIn or YouTube is also subject to the relevant platform’s privacy practices.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers, logistics partners, manufacturers or authorities only where reasonably necessary to fulfil a request, provide service or comply with law. Records are retained only as long as reasonably required for these purposes.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information, subject to business and legal recordkeeping requirements. Contact us at chanderiya.group@gmail.com or +91 90273 98484.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
