Shipping & Delivery Policy

How delivery arrangements are confirmed for institutional products and equipment.

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Delivery coverage

Delivery availability depends on product type, quantity, destination and installation requirements. Meeting and service coordination is available from Haridwar, Dehradun and Kotdwar; supply to other locations may be arranged as confirmed in writing.

Charges and timelines

Freight, handling, unloading, installation and insurance charges, if applicable, will be stated in the quotation. Delivery estimates begin after order acceptance, required payment and approval of specifications. Custom furniture and special-order equipment may require longer lead times.

Receiving an order

The customer should ensure safe site access, unloading arrangements where agreed, and an authorized person to inspect and acknowledge delivery. Visible shortages or transit damage should be recorded on the delivery document and reported promptly with photographs.

Delays

We communicate known delays and work to provide a revised estimate. Timelines may be affected by manufacturer availability, transport disruption, weather, access restrictions, public holidays or events beyond reasonable control.

Installation

Installation or commissioning is included only when stated in the quotation. Site readiness, utilities, permissions and safe working access remain the customer’s responsibility unless otherwise agreed.
