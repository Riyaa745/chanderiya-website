Cancellation & Refund Policy

General guidance for cancellations, returns and service concerns.

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Because many products are sourced, configured or manufactured to institutional requirements, cancellation and return eligibility depends on the applicable quotation and order status.

Cancellations

Submit cancellation requests in writing as early as possible. Orders already processed, dispatched, installed, specially procured or manufactured to specification may not be cancellable. Reasonable costs already incurred may be deducted from any approved refund.

Returns and damaged products

Report incorrect, defective or transit-damaged products promptly after delivery, with the invoice, delivery details, photographs and a description of the issue. Do not install or use a visibly damaged product unless advised. We will assess whether repair, replacement, credit or another remedy is appropriate.

Non-returnable items

Customized or made-to-order furniture, opened safety products, used consumables, installed equipment and products damaged after delivery are generally not returnable except where defective or otherwise required by applicable law.

Service concerns

If a repair or maintenance service does not meet the agreed scope, notify us promptly so the work can be reviewed. Any revisit or remedy will depend on the service findings, replaced parts and agreed terms.

Approved refunds

Approved refunds are issued through a mutually agreed method after required inspection and documentation. Processing time may vary by bank or payment channel.
